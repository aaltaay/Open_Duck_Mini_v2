- D15 Servo power bypasses the adapter. DECIDED 6:48 PM ET: Castle CC BEC Pro -> 16 AWG -> V+ and GND splices in the belly -> three 22 AWG power leads, one per chain (SP1 left leg at ID 20, SP2 right leg at ID 10, SP3 neck at ID 30). The adapter carries only DATA and GND (H3 to ID 20 and, through a data-only Y, ID 30; H5 to ID 10); its H1 screw terminal is not connected. S1, S6 and S14 are replaced. Reasons: the Waveshare schematic marks "MAX:6A" at the servo header and the adapter is specced for 9 to 12.6 V input (parts check), while the sim BEC output peaks at 8.3 to 9.1 A; the adapter logic runs from USB VBUS (schematic). Power sim at a nominal pack: worst ankle 5.04 V (typical corner) vs 4.78 V with the Y-lead, head servos at 5.0 V or more in both corners. Supersedes: D14, the Tnkr neck wiring (B19), the screw-terminal feed in WIRING.md.
- D16 Battery cut-off and BEC low input. DECIDED 6:55 PM ET: the Tenergy board only cuts at 2.40 V per cell (below the P30B 2.5 V minimum) and Castle does not publish the CC BEC Pro minimum input. So: (a) software stop of walking at about 3.5 V per cell under load and hard stop at servo Present Voltage 6.0 V (about 3.3 V per cell); (b) the BEC is swept from 8.4 V down to 6.5 V on the bench before assembly (TEST_PLAN.md section 4); if it shuts off instead of passing the input through, use the Pololu D42V110F6 (6 V fixed) from POWER.md section 3.

- D17 Foot switch contacts. DECIDED 7:24 PM ET: the Runtime feeds feet_contacts into the policy observation (scripts/v2_rl_walk_mujoco.py lines 154 and 167), so contact reliability matters for walking. Omron SS datasheet (en-ss.pdf): SS-10 silver contacts need a minimum load of 5 VDC 160 mA; SS-01 gold alloy crossbar contacts need 5 VDC 1 mA (0.1 A rating, same housing).
