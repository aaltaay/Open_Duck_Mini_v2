- D16 note, 2026-11-01 10:50 PM ET (from the software agent's battery guard, software/SOFTWARE.md section 5 and bench/battery_guard.py): (a) cannot be applied literally, because while walking the 14-servo median Present Voltage reads 6.0 V at about 3.57 V per cell (sim pass only, bench/sim_profiles.py).
