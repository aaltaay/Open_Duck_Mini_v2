# Proposed decision, for Ahmed to approve

Not entered in DECISIONS.md. D61 stands as written. This text is the successor, if he wants it.

## Proposed: nest mat version 2

The cute sit stays. The duck charges while sitting, torque off, head up, facing the room. The mat stays against the wall. Finding stays camera only.

Drop the four spring pins under the body. They are the awkward part. Nothing sticks out of the duck. The feet stay plain. No wire is added down a leg.

The mat is a felt rug, 2 mm, with two 1 mm steps at the edge (the stations already scored, 247 mm and 267 mm from the sun). The sun stays 190 mm from the wall. The contact is a flush striped field: 20 mm foil, 5 mm felt, five 3 mm gold wells in the seat. The seat may land anywhere inside +/-254 mm along the wall and +/-161 mm deep, up to 46 deg, and still have one well on each pole. That window is the one already calculated in the flush study.

On the stored 540 seeds (sim pass only, not hardware): the blind walk puts the first seat in that window in 380 of 540, with 0 falls. The same walk with the 40 mm wall card does it in 533 of 540, with 1 fall. The true-pose walk does it in 540 of 540. The old pins do it in 35 of 540 blind.

The 40 mm card is a plain square on the wall at 350 mm, not a coded tag. It is still a mark. D61 said no markers. This proposal keeps the mat either way, and asks Ahmed to say yes or no to the card. Without it, the first-sit count is 380 of 540.

The charger stays the MCP73213 board already drawn, in the duck, on the 2S P45B pack, 8.40 V, 1.00 A, 6 hour timer. The mat is a gated supply into that board. Awake while docked stays.

Not taken, and why, in one line each:

- Magnetic pads: the largest housing on the sheet that was read is 16.5 mm, and the capture distance is not published. That face holds 5 of 540 blind seats.
- Inductive, including Qi2 15 W and Qi2 25W: a phone coil's 10 mm offset holds 3 of 540 blind seats, and 15 W does not cover the awake-while-docked peak. 25 W after loss is not on the page that was opened.
- A cradle or wedge: a low slope does not slide the foot, and a tall guide is a step the walk already trips on.
- Springs in the mat: a field dense enough for the 3 mm wells is about 45,000 pins. A few pins at the centre are the old small target.

Open, and not claimed as done: contact resistance with dirt, the gate board, belly diodes for either stripe, charger heat at the new watt figures, a real camera detector, and whether the card as a solid plate is clear of the head.

Nothing in this proposal was run on hardware.
