## D67 candidate: full belly, nest mat version 3

Approved by Ahmed 2026-11-16 7:24 PM ET. Redesign the mat so the charging contact is the full metal belly, split into two conductive halves.

What was tried (altaay_lab/cad/nest_v3/NEST_V3.md): the halves are the whole seat and the whole bumper, because a left-right split cannot cover the along-wall scatter this walk actually produces, and the walk does not side-step. The mat is two pads with a 24 mm felt strip parallel to the wall. The ideal-diode bridge stays, so a 180 deg sit still feeds the charger the right way. The pads stay at a few volts until a sense resistor on the belly is seen.

Result, sim pass only, same 40 camera-dock seeds: 15 geometric docks, 15 charging docks, 4 shorts. The gold domes are 38 of 40 with no short in that earlier batch. 15 is not better than 38. The gold wells stay the contact that charges more often. This approval is the order to try the redesign. It is not a finding that the redesign won.
