# TWIN CHECKLIST: gate 0

Written 2026-11-11 ET. This is the end-to-end digital twin checklist for gate 0 in DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md.
Gate 0 passes only when every row below is PASS and the rows describe one duck. A sim pass only, a calc only,
or a CAD check only result is evidence. It is not a PASS of the row.

The duck is not printed, the pack is not welded, and the full order is not placed before this checklist is all PASS. Today it is not.

Characterization coupons are allowed, and only after Ahmed says yes. A row that needs a measurement names the coupon. For the pins that is TEST_PLAN.md 20.1 to 20.4: a few pins, a scrap of cloth, and a scrap of felt. No whole duck. The coupon is not the product and it is not the one-click bill. It exists so a guess in this list can be replaced by a measurement.

How to update a row: change the status word, the labeled result, the guess, and the next action together.
Leave the old wording in a new history file. Do not edit this file's history by silent overwrite of a result
that was wrong; write the new result in the row and say what changed.

Status words, only these:

- PASS: the pass criterion is met for the duck that will be built.
- FAIL: a run or a check exists, and it does not meet the criterion.
- NOT RUN: the check is defined and has not been executed.
- GAP: the model, the part, or the file that would hold the check does not exist yet.

## Thermal (twin/thermal)

- Pass: the heat model is the duck that will be built, including the charger inside the body (D61), the P45B cells, and the STS3250 knees, and the temperatures stay inside the datasheets (servo operating range, cell charge window, PLA).

Docked sit, torque off, charger 1.9 to 3.2 W in the trunk for 6 h (the watts are calc only, D61). Cells at 6 h: 31.5 C and 33.7 C in a 25 C room, 38.3 C and 40.4 C in a 32 C room, and 42.4 C in the pessimistic corner at 32 C and 3.2 W. All are inside the P45B charge window of 0 C to 60 C (version 1.2). Servo readings end at 27.6 C to 34.7 C, inside the STS3215 operating range of -20 C to 60 C (sheet A/1, item 1-2). The trunk wall, the PLA this model has for trunk heat because the charger has no seat, ends at 28.6 C to 37.6 C, under the 49 C to 57 C heat-deflection band in THERMAL_TWIN.md. The walk uses the P45B session RMS currents of the trained actuator, because the sheet-stall servo does not walk. The right knee reading reaches 60 C at 3.3 minutes in a 25 C room and 2.5 minutes at 32 C. The same sheet turns torque off above 70 C (item 7-11); that reading is at 4.5 and 3.5 minutes. If the torque stays on, the right knee body reaches 94.7 C and 101.6 C, which is what the knee sheets touch, above the softening band. Cells in the central walk reach 49.7 C and 56.4 C. The pessimistic corner at 32 C reaches 63.9 C at the cells at 33 minutes if the walk is not cut.
- Guess: BEC efficiency is 0.85. Castle publishes none. Cell specific heat is the old 18650 range, not on the P45B sheet. Half the can faces the bay air. The charger is not a seated part. Walk friction heat is not in the session bins. STS3250 knees are not in this run. The earlier line that said the 70 C torque-off is not on the STS3215 sheet was wrong: item 7-11 has it. The operating range is still -20 C to 60 C.
- Next: the 1.9 to 3.2 W charger can stay in the trunk on this model during a sit. Do not treat the walk as inside the servo limit. Seat the charger before calling the wall temperature a gap to a board. Open the STS3250 sheet before swapping the knee heat (TEST_PLAN.md 7.3 and 20.8 still measure the servo).

## Electromechanical (twin/electromech)

- Pass: one closed loop of the walk, the 14 servos, and the as-built power path, using the servo torque and speed from the datasheet and the P45B pack, matches the duck that will be built across the battery range.
- Status: FAIL.
- Result: sim pass only, ELECTROMECH_TWIN.md update, out/p45b_sheet/. The pack is the P45B figure (version 1.2). With the sheet stall (19.5 kg.cm at 7.4 V) on the as-built bus, the backlash walk falls at 3.52 s (typical) and 3.46 s (high-side), and the plain walk at 3.56 s. From 4 s to 20 s the speed is about 0.04 of the command. The same sheet model held at a flat 7.4 V does not fall in 20 s and walks at 0.64 of the command. With the trained actuator and the P45B pack, no fall in the state runs or in two sessions. Full charge, typical: pack 8.17 V at 5.30 A mean (5.61 A RMS, 10.5 A peak), sag 0.18 V from an 8.35 V open circuit. Nominal: 7.21 V at 5.33 A, speed 0.69 of the command. The typical session reaches an open circuit of 3.30 V per cell at 42.9 minutes, the walk drops under 85 percent of its full-charge speed at 25.7 minutes, and the guard walk stop is at 34.5 minutes (29.5 minutes high-side). Those trained-actuator numbers are not the sheet-stall duck. The 2026-11-03 no-fall line is the P30B run.
- Guess: sheet damping and friction are the training values scaled with the stall (no-load speed 5.65 against 5.45 rad/s at 7.4 V, inside the sheet's +/-10 percent). Whether the firmware holds stiffness as the bus falls is unknown (TEST_PLAN.md 7.5). The 0 mAh end of the discharge figure is extended back to the axis. The figure slope is 13 mOhm at an open circuit of 3.70 V; the sheet's DC pulse is 15 mOhm at 50 percent SOC.
- Next: do not quote the trained-actuator walk as the servo that will be bought. The sheet stall on the as-built bus falls. A bench stall and a gain measurement come before another closed-loop claim.

## What blocks gate 0, in order

1. There is no single model. The print set, the netlist, and the viewer are the walking duck. The nest is an archive beside them. Until those are one duck, gate 0 cannot pass.
2. The bill does not match that duck and cannot be ordered today (PARTS_AUDIT.md). A one-click of today's bill buys the old chest pad and misses the nest.
3. The camera, the felt, the cloth, and the pin contact are not models. The nest sims are a pose stand-in on a rigid mat. That cannot stand in for gate 0.
4. The charger sit was re-run. On this model the cells, the servos, and the trunk wall stay inside their windows during a docked charge (sim pass only). A walk still leaves the 60 C operating range, and the knee sheets see a body temperature above the softening band. The STS3215 sheet has both: operating range -20 C to 60 C, and torque off above 70 C.
5. The electromechanical loop runs the P45B figure. With the sheet stall the walk falls (sim pass only). The trained-actuator session (42.9 minutes, no fall) is not that servo. The electrical fall and get-up replay was not re-run: those traces are not in this repo (BLOCKER).
6. The welded pack has no part number and no model. The opened cell maximum fits the sleeved bay (22.2 by 43.7 by 71.0 mm, CAD check only, twin/battery/bay_edit.py). The electromechanical twin now points at the P45B figure.
7. Structure is under the lab margin on roll_to_pitch and the knee sheet eye (FEA pass only). D26 is open. Hot PLA makes that worse in the same file.
8. Get-up misses 48 down-ramp front rests (sim pass only). The stock policy falls on the STS3250 knee model in sim. Neither is a shipping behavior.
9. The nest sims still fall on the mat edge (29 of 540 steering, 13 of 540 straight, sim pass only). A walk change needs a decision and, if it is a retrain, a spend approval. It is not a substitute for items 1 to 3.
