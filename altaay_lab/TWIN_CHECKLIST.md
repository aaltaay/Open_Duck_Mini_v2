# TWIN CHECKLIST: gate 0

Written 2026-11-11 ET. This is the end-to-end digital twin checklist for gate 0 in DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md.
Gate 0 passes only when every row below is PASS and the rows describe one duck. A sim pass only, a calc only,
or a CAD check only result is evidence. It is not a PASS of the row.

Nothing physical is built before this checklist is all PASS. Today it is not.

How to update a row: change the status word, the labeled result, the guess, and the next action together.
Leave the old wording in a new history file. Do not edit this file's history by silent overwrite of a result
that was wrong; write the new result in the row and say what changed.

Status words, only these:

- PASS: the pass criterion is met for the duck that will be built.
- FAIL: a run or a check exists, and it does not meet the criterion.
- NOT RUN: the check is defined and has not been executed.
- GAP: the model, the part, or the file that would hold the check does not exist yet.

## Structure (twin/structure)

- Pass: every printed part that carries a walking, push, or fall load holds the lab margin (2 walking, 1.2 in a fall), with the nest's extra mass and the sit stops included, and the material numbers come from a datasheet or a coupon.
- Status: FAIL.
- Result: walking, flat floor, room temperature, FEA pass only on sim pass only loads. roll_to_pitch safety factor 1.48 to 1.63. The knee sheet eye 1.63. Both are under 2 (twin/structure/STRUCTURE_TWIN.md, answer). Soft falls put roll_to_pitch under 1.2 in 7 of 10 falls, the neck sheets in 9 of 10. Hot case about 1.0 at 50 C. D26 is still open.
- Guess: contact stiffness is a range, not a measurement. PLA strength versus temperature is a cited curve, not a coupon from this printer. The nest, the sit stops, and the STS3250 knees are not the model this file ran (the knee twin scaled it later).
- Next: do not treat this file as the freeze structure. Re-run after the nest mass, the sit parts, and the P45B pack are in the same model, and only after D26 is decided.

## Thermal (twin/thermal)

- Pass: the heat model is the duck that will be built, including the charger inside the body (D61), the P45B cells, and the STS3250 knees, and the temperatures stay inside the datasheets (servo operating range, cell charge window, PLA).
- Status: FAIL.
- Result: without that charger, continuous walking, central set, sim pass only. The hotter knee reaches a 70 C cut after about 10 minutes (plain model) or 4 minutes (backlash model) at a 25 C room (twin/thermal/THERMAL_TWIN.md). Trunk electronics 6 to 9 W while walking. PLA on the knee sheets 54 to 66 C, against a heat-deflection band of 49 to 57 C in that file. The D61 charger re-run is NOT RUN.
- Guess: the 70 C cut is not on the STS3215 sheet opened for PARTS_AUDIT.md. That sheet's operating range is -20 C to 60 C. BEC efficiency is assumed (85 percent in the central set). Castle publishes no efficiency in the sources that file could read. Charger heat of 1.9 to 3.2 W is calc only (D61). The electronics paragraph still quotes a P30B discharge limit.
- Next: re-run twin/thermal with the charger in the trunk, the opened P45B charge window (0 C to 60 C), and the servo limit taken from the opened sheet, not from the 70 C assumption, until a measurement replaces it (TEST_PLAN.md 7.3 and 20.8).

## Electromechanical (twin/electromech)

- Pass: one closed loop of the walk, the 14 servos, and the as-built power path, using the servo torque and speed from the datasheet and the P45B pack, matches the duck that will be built across the battery range.
- Status: FAIL.
- Result: the loop was run, sim pass only, on a 2S P30B pack and the BAM servo model. No fall in 85 closed-loop runs in the training physics. At a nominal pack the backlash-model walk is 79 percent of trained speed (typical corner) and 76 percent (high-side). Knees under 6.0 V about 4 to 5 percent of the walk, typical, nominal pack (twin/electromech/ELECTROMECH_TWIN.md). Those runs are not the P45B duck and not the nest.
- Guess: servo stall in that model is about 3.23 Nm at 7.4 V. The opened STS3215 sheet says 19.5 kg.cm at 7.4 V, which is a lower stall. Whether the firmware holds stiffness as the bus falls is unknown (TEST_PLAN.md 7.5). Rotor inertia is not measured. The pack is the wrong cell for D44.
- Next: rebuild the loop on the P45B curves from the opened sheet (version 1.2) and on the sheet stall, then say what the walk does. Do not quote the 85-run no-fall line as the P45B duck.

- Next: draw the two-net circuit, put it in the netlist, and re-run the electrical twin. Until then the V1 "nothing fails" line is the old dock's power path, not the nest.

## Nest dock (twin/dock/nest)

- Pass: the installed model walks onto the real felt shape, turns 180 deg, sits, connects four pins, charges, and leaves, with the camera in the loop, and the CAD, the netlist, and the bill are that same nest.
- Status: FAIL.
- Result: two sequence sims, sim pass only. Steering walk-on: first sit charges in 93.1 percent of 540 runs (503), 29 falls, all at 7.2 V, all on the mat edge (twin/dock/nest/sim/NEST_SIM.md). Pin tip diameter in the CAD (about 2.3 mm) is not on the QA Technology 100-40 catalog page opened for PARTS_AUDIT.md. Round tips on that page are 0.91 mm and 1.40 mm. Stroke 10.16 mm and the standard spring (0.42 N, then 1.58 N at 8.05 mm) do match that catalog.
- Next: do not install the CAD on a guessed tip. Pick the tip code, then decide the look, then land one model. The edge falls are a walk problem (toe lift, a right side-step), not a reason to ignore the rest of this row.

## What blocks gate 0, in order

1. There is no single model. The print set, the netlist, and the viewer are the walking duck. The nest is an archive beside them. Until those are one duck, gate 0 cannot pass.
2. The bill does not match that duck and cannot be ordered today (PARTS_AUDIT.md). A one-click of today's bill buys the old chest pad and misses the nest.
3. The camera, the felt, the cloth, and the pin contact are not models. The nest sims are a pose stand-in on a rigid mat. That cannot stand in for gate 0.
4. The heat twin has not been re-run with the charger inside the duck. The 70 C cut used there is not the operating range on the opened STS3215 sheet (-20 C to 60 C).
5. The electromechanical and electrical twins still run the older P30B pack and a servo stall the opened STS3215 sheet does not give. They are not the P45B duck.
6. The cell bay is drawn at 21.40 mm by 70.00 mm. The opened P45B sheet says 21.55 mm and 70.15 mm maximum. The welded pack has no part number and no model.
7. Structure is under the lab margin on roll_to_pitch and the knee sheet eye (FEA pass only). D26 is open. Hot PLA makes that worse in the same file.
8. Get-up misses 48 down-ramp front rests (sim pass only). The stock policy falls on the STS3250 knee model in sim. Neither is a shipping behavior.
9. The nest sims still fall on the mat edge (29 of 540 steering, 13 of 540 straight, sim pass only). A walk change needs a decision and, if it is a retrain, a spend approval. It is not a substitute for items 1 to 3.
