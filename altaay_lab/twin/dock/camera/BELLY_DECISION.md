## Proposed: do not replace the gold wells with the belly halves

The 0.9 mm gold domes charge in 38 of 40 camera docks (sim pass only, SEAT_CONTACTS.md). The belly inserts charge in 18 of 40 on the same seeds (sim pass only, BELLY_CONTACT.md). 18 is not as good as 38. Do not swap the wells out.

The inserts are the right shape for a short: 0 of 4,100 phase and heading checks bridge a plus stripe and a minus stripe, including a print that is 0.3 mm fat (calc only). They also press hard when they do land, down to 0.77 N on a charging sit, over the 0.294 N floor. The miss is the stripe phase. Two pads one period apart both sit in the 5 mm felt gap together. The five wells were spaced so that does not happen. A metal half the full width of the seat would short the mat, so the shell cannot just be split in two and plated.

The bumper had to be raised 4 mm in the model so the seat inserts could touch. Four docks that stood with the gold domes tipped over in this model (seeds 3, 25, 35, 38). That is another reason not to take this as the build.

Leave the gold domes as the duck-side contact until a bench, or a later layout with more than two isolated pads, beats 38 of 40 without a short. This proposal does not claim that layout.

Nothing here was run on hardware.
