## 4. D12 camera cable as a flat strip (ribbon_d12.py)

Model:
- The strip bends only about its width axis and turns in its own plane only by a flat fold. No twist.
- Ends:
  - Pi Zero 2 W: 22-pin 0.5 mm FPC on the Pi short edge, exit -x, at (1.6, 48.35, 223.4) (cables3d PI.CSI, from the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W mechanical drawing).
  - Camera: 15-pin 1 mm FPC at the designed bottom edge, entering +y with the width along z.
  - Pin counts and pitches: BOM E7 and CABLE_MAP D12.
- Width BLOCKER: the repo stand-in is 16 mm at the 15-pin end and 11.5 mm at the 22-pin end. Lower bound from the pitch, calc only: 14 and 10.5 mm between the outer contact centres.
- Thickness BLOCKER: stand-in 0.3 mm.
- Bend and fold rule BLOCKER: none published at a reachable source. Bends are 3 mm and up; folds are flat, not creased.

Search (clearance is CAD check only; length is calc only). The obstacles are every head part, the head placeholders, the small scene_extras parts and the designed camera PCB.
- 16 mm along the whole length: 9,520 routes, none clears.
  - Under the Pi, the head servo case (servo_top on the head link) blocks at about (46, 12, 203).
  - Over the Pi, the head shell behind the Pi blocks the U bend at about (-0.4, 42, 230).
- Stand-in taper (11.5 mm, 16 mm on the last 20 mm): 11,016 routes. The shortest with 1.0 mm or more on the exact meshes is 227.4 mm (worst 1.08 mm, to the 5 V regulator box). The route:
  1. A 180 degree bend of radius 12 behind the Pi, then a step down to z 181 (the head floor).
  2. A diagonal under the servo between two 53 degree folds.
  3. Along +x to beside the camera.
  4. A 90 degree bend up (radius 3), a 33 mm rise in the board plane, and a 90 degree fold into the connector.
- The round tube route is 127 mm only because a tube turns any way. A strip that may not twist has to go round by the floor.

Length verdict (calc only, 40 mm slack rule from check_cables.py SLACK_MM):
- 150 mm, Adafruit 3157 (BOM E7, https://www.adafruit.com/product/3157, $5.95): 77 mm short. Fail.
- 200 mm, Adafruit 5818, Raspberry Pi Standard-Mini, 22-pin 0.5 mm to 15-pin 1 mm, $2.70, in stock (https://www.adafruit.com/product/5818, read 2026-11-02 1:46 AM ET, BOM E7 note, MPN SC1892): 27 mm short of the no-twist route, before any slack. Fail in this model.
- So the 200 mm cable that Ahmed approved (D31) reaches only if the strip may twist along a free span. Real camera cables are routed that way, but no reachable source gives a twist rule: BLOCKER, and the TO9 test fit settles it. The fixes, in order:
  - (a) TO9 test fit of the 200 mm cable along the tube corridor with one twist. If it fits, the spare (about 200 - 127 mm, less the twist length) is stored in one standing loop: radius 3, two 90 degree bends and one 180 degree bend, on the longest straight piece. ribbon_d12.py has the loop code; the no-twist route has no spare to store.
  - (b) A 300 mm Raspberry Pi Standard-Mini cable. Price and link BLOCKER (adafruit.com is not reachable here). Needs Ahmed's OK.
  - (c) Turn the Pi 180 degrees about its board normal, so its CSI edge faces the camera. The straight line to the camera connector is then 65.6 mm (calc only). This moves the header and the USB port, so P12, P17 and D1 to D10 re-route, and the head_board pilots must match after the turn. Lead decision.
- The strip route crosses P13, D6 and D8 (centre line gap under half the strip width). Those are notes, not failures.
