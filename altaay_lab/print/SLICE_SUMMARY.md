The old run was reproduced first: PrusaSlicer 2.7.2 defaults plus 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls, 5 top and 4 bottom layers, gyroid 15%, grid supports at 45 degrees and density 1.24 gives the old grams to 0.01 g on 5 parts (battery_pack_lid, body_back, head_yaw_to_roll, trunk_top, foot_bottom_tpu) and the old hours within 0.5%.
- More grams: 3 walls instead of 2 on every part, 4 walls on the 7 insert parts, 5 walls and 40% infill on the 5 neck chain parts (13.0 g of it).
- Fewer grams: PLA density 1.20 (Overture TDS) instead of 1.24 (-3%), and supports only below 40 degrees in snug style: 90.4 g of support in all, against 208.1 g with the old settings (old settings re-run on all parts here: 1334.0 g in all, PRINT_CHECK has 1339.0 g).
- Fewer PLA hours: solid infill at 60 mm/s and top infill at 40 mm/s instead of the PrusaSlicer defaults of 20 and 15 mm/s.
- More TPU hours: TPU at 25 mm/s (maker range 20 to 40) instead of the 60 to 80 mm/s PLA speeds the old run used for it.
- speaker_interface now lies flat (2.33 g, 0.22 h) instead of standing on a 48 mm2 edge (4.56 g, 0.61 h).
