# Board note, recommendation only

Written 2026-11-19. The BOM is not changed. Nothing was bought. A number is cited or BLOCKER. This is not a fit measurement.

Note, 2026-11-24: Ahmed chose the Pi 5, 2 GB, and a new body seat. The study, with a proposed BOM delta, is software/pi_fit/PI5_STUDY.md. This note is the earlier comparison. It is not the decision.

The duck body is laid out for the Pi Zero 2 W, 65 mm by 30 mm (cited, https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-zero-2-w/). A taller board does not drop into that seat. FIT.md is why this note exists: the published Bookworm Lite image does not leave enough RAM for the stack.

| Board | RAM | Size | Power | Price | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pi Zero 2 W | 512 MB SDRAM (cited, the product page above) | 65 mm by 30 mm (cited, same page) | 0.6 W to 1.2 W minimum (cited, Raspberry Pi whitepaper RP-010139-WP, table 1). A separate idle reading on Bullseye Lite was about 120 mA at 5 V, which is 0.6 W (cited, https://www.cnx-software.com/2021/12/09/raspberry-pi-zero-2-w-power-consumption/). A wall idle of 0.7 W and a stress peak of 3 W are a different setup (cited, https://github.com/geerlingguy/sbc-reviews/issues/43) | Launch price $15 (cited, https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/new-raspberry-pi-zero-2-w-2/, 28 Oct 2021). A current Approved Reseller price was not on the product page text read for this note: BLOCKER | The board in the build. FIT.md says the stack does not fit its Bookworm Lite memory map |

Idle watts were not in that brief: BLOCKER | Launch range $15 for 1 GB with no eMMC up to $65 for 8 GB with 64 GB eMMC (cited, https://www.notebookcheck.net/Radxa-Zero-3W-Raspberry-Pi-Zero-2-W-alternative-debuts-with-faster-chip-up-to-8-GB-RAM.763526.0.html, 30 Oct 2023). Idle watts: BLOCKER | From $67.50 on the product page (cited, the page above). The 2 GB lite is listed at $67.50 (cited, the PiShop page and https://www.seeedstudio.com/Raspberry-Pi-Compute-Module-CM5002000-p-6271.html). The 1 Oct 2026 price note says the 2 GB CM5 price was not raised | Enough RAM, and smaller than a Pi 5, but it needs a carrier board the duck does not have. Not measured here |

Recommendation, not a purchase: if the body can keep a 65 mm by 30 mm board, a Radxa ZERO 3W with 2 GB is the one that matches the seat and the RAM. A Pi 5 solves the RAM and does not match the seat. A CM5 solves the RAM and needs a new carrier. None of these rows is a measured walk on that board.
