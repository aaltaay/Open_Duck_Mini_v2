Screw rule: choose the length so the thread end stays inside the pilot (length less than the window far end) and engages at least 3 mm (length minus window near end). All electronics pilots are 2.0 mm printed holes, which an M2.5 machine screw self-taps into (no insert, no nut, no washer; no nut traps or washer seats exist in the STLs). The one washer use is a spacer: two 0.3 mm washers under each eye screw head, because the eye pilots are blind (D21 and its note).
