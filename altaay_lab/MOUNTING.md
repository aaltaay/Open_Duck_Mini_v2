# MOUNTING

Last updated: 2026-11-01 6:57 PM ET (placement pass, first at 6:20 PM ET for parts not in the CAD; earlier: 2:52 PM ET blocker pass). Measured on the upstream assembly (URDF meshes + print STLs, STL commit 730c14b) with trimesh: for each modelled board, rays were cast down each hole to find the printed part under it, the pilot diameter, and where the pilot starts and ends below the board top face ("engagement window"). Scripts: audit/screws2.py, audit/sites.py; data: audit/screws2.json, audit/screws_head.json.

Screw rule: choose the length so the thread end stays inside the pilot (length less than the window far end) and engages at least 3 mm (length minus window near end). All electronics pilots are 2.0 mm printed holes, which an M2.5 machine screw self-taps into (no insert, no nut, no washer; no nut traps or washer seats exist in the STLs).
