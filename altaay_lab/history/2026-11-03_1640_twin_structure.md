- What: twin/structure/STRUCTURE_TWIN.md (the answer, loads, falls, FEA, fasteners, fatigue, hot case, weak list and proposals), tables.md, and the scripts st_sim.py, st_loads.py, st_falls.py, st_interfaces.py, st_fea.py, st_fea_run.py, st_report.py, st_finalize.py, st_slice_proposals.py. Heavy outputs (time series, meshes, G-code) stay in the session scratch folder twinST.
- Loads (sim pass only, MuJoCo 3.17.0, stock BEST_WALK_ONNX_2, our 2652.8 g build): force and torque sensors at all 14 joints and accelerometers at every link; 16 walking runs, 100 single pushes and the 15 N push series, with 30 Newton iterations (the upstream 1 iteration leaves the contact forces unconverged: ground force spikes of 133 to 194 N). Our build raises the walking peaks by a median 19 % (0 to 60 %) over the trained mass (st_loads.py trained). With 2 or more iterations our build falls in 4 of 4 runs of the 15 N push case; with 1 it falls in none (for the sim owner; the shared gym found the same).
- Falls: printed-part collision added, replayed at 0.1 ms over a contact stiffness range; "soft" (a wall hit, 0.14 to 0.34 MN/m) gives 400 to 900 N and 15 to 71 g at the head, "mid" (an edge on a hard floor, 1.35 to 3.2 MN/m) 1300 to 2800 N and 54 to 196 g.
- FEA (FEA pass only): TetGen linear tetrahedra, as-printed zones from the D22 profiles, cited effective properties, a failure index in each part's print frame, 1 mm stress averaging, unit loads superposed over every sim sample. Seven studies at mesh level 1; roll_to_pitch at levels 1 to 3 and the shin at levels 1 and 2 (the knee sheet alone at 1 to 3). Walking SF: roll_to_pitch 1.48 to 1.63 (push 1.08 to 1.21), the knee sheet eye of the shin 1.63 (push 1.07), the thigh sheet 2.70 (1.74), foot_top 5.5, the neck sheets 5.4, the hip roll stage 12.9, head_roll_mount 48. Print orientation of roll_to_pitch: both alternatives are worse; 3 walls (its profile before D22) gives 1.84 / 1.25 at level 1 for +7.2 g and +0.44 h (both legs); knee sheets 5 walls 40 %: 1.82 / 1.20.
- Fasteners: walking holds every horn and case screw over 2 except the ankle case screws (1.6); push recovery takes the knee drive horn screws to 1.2 to 1.6 and the knee and ankle case screws to 1.1 to 1.9. Inserts walking SF 12 or more. Self-tapping pull-out in printed pilots: no data (BLOCKER).
- Fatigue: 6667 load cycles per hour per leg. Design curve (Ezeh and Susmel 2018, P_s 95 %): roll_to_pitch and the knee sheet eye last under 1 hour; best-case data (Gomez-Gras et al. 2018): no failure, ratio 1.2. Life: BLOCKER, needs a rig test.
- Hot case (the coordinator's request, from the thermal twin): at 50 C printed PLA keeps about 0.62 of its strength, so both weak parts are at about 1.0 walking and 0.7 in push; strength at 60 C and creep rates are BLOCKERS; standing stress 3.4 to 4.3 MPa, 2 to 10 x the HDT test stress, so creep is expected above about 50 C. PETG (HDT 69 C) holds about 1.1 to 1.3 walking to about 60 C (ASSUMPTION), not 2.
- Checks made late in the session: the stress averaging radius (0.5, 1 and 1.5 mm: walking and push verdicts unchanged); foot_top first stalled in CG (relative residual 2e-2, a 0.003 mm STL edge made 70 % of the tets tiny) and was re-run with the STL edges under 0.01 mm collapsed (manifold3d 3.5.4), residual 9.8e-9; the level 3 roll_to_pitch run was made on its own (scratch fea_conv3/, same code) to save time.
