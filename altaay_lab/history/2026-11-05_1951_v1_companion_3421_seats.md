# V1 track 11: the 3421 mic board read, the seats redrawn on the real port (CAD check only)

2026-11-05 about 7:51 PM ET (America/New_York). Adds to the track 11 entries; they are not edited.

Read with plain curl, each into its own folder outside the repo:
- adafruit.com/product/3421: $6.95 each (1 to 9), in stock; 16.7 x 12.7 x 1.8 mm overall, 0.4 g.
- The 3421 board files (github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-I2S-Microphone-Breakout-PCB, EagleCAD, files named "Adafruit I2S Mic
  SPK0415HM4H", README links product 3421) and the SPH0645 fab print in the Adafruit guide.
- The Knowles SPH0645LM4H-B Rev B datasheet (the PDF linked on the product page).

Compared with the 6049 stand-in the seats were drawn from:
- outline 16.51 x 12.7 mm, holes 2.5 mm at (2.54, 10.16) and (13.97, 10.16), footprint centre (8.255, 7.112): the same;
- the sound port: the PCB hole of footprint pad 3 (GND, drilled 1.025 mm) sits 0.71 mm from the mic centre, at
  (8.255, 7.822) (fab print: "Port" 0.31 in above the pad edge); the old tube and wall port were centred on 7.112, so
  the 1.025 mm PCB hole stood partly behind the tube wall. Fixed: the tube and the wall port now sit on the real port;
- PCB thickness: not published; the 1.8 mm overall height with the 0.98 mm mic points to about 0.8 mm (calc only).
  At 0.8 mm the M2x6 tip would have passed the 5.0 mm pilot by 0.2 mm, so the bosses are 3.5 mm high now (was 3.0)
  with 5.6 mm pilots: engagement 4.4 to 5.2 mm, tip gap 0.4 to 1.2 mm, 0.9 mm of wall past the pilot, for 0.8 to
  1.6 mm boards. D42 and D39 kept (mirror pair per board, both holes used, the port is a sound hole).
- The seat search then picked x = -37.5 mm (was -40): SG90 clearance 6.05 mm (was 3.46), mic spacing 121.6 mm (was
  120.5). D14 and D15 routed again (78.3 and 148.4 mm), fasteners F356 to F359 and the viewer boxes updated
  (twin/companion/mic_fasteners.py), the section and seat close-ups rendered and looked at.
- Datasheet facts now in COMPANION.md: clock 2.048 to 4.096 MHz, AOP 120 dB SPL, SNR 65 dB(A), sensitivity -26 dBFS,
  600 uA, specifications guaranteed 0 to 45 C (the thermal twin's head air is 41 C in a 32 C room, sim pass only).
- D47 BLOCKERs 1 cleared and 2 mostly cleared (PCB thickness and part-to-part group delay still open); D49 BLOCKER 4
  (AOP) cleared; BOM E8 verified on the page.
