# Charger board schematic

2026-11-11 11:17 PM ET (America/New_York).

Schematic of the in-body charger, in `altaay_lab/circuitry/`. The MCP73213 sheet (DS20002190C, revision C, December 2014) and the Molicel INR21700-P45B sheet (version 1.2) were opened. The eval board guide DS51849A is the reference circuit: 4.7 µF on the input and the output, and a resistor on PROG.

The order code is MCP73213-A6SI/MF. Table 2's A6S option is 8.40 V, 6 hour timer, 10 percent termination. The I is the industrial temperature letter in the ordering key. Charge current is 1.00 A typical from a 1.10 kOhm resistor (equation 5-1 gives 1010.4 mA, inside the 900 to 1100 mA window). That is 0.222 C of the cell's 4.5 A standard charge.

At the D61 figure of 9.5 V, 1.00 A is 2.30 W at a 7.2 V pack and 3.50 W at a 6.0 V pack (calc). The older band was 1.9 W and 3.2 W, which implies about 9.1 V and 9.2 V if the current is 1 A. The die's thermal regulation (110 C on the block diagram, 62 C/W on the sheet's standard board) will cut the current when that heat is too high. Whether a folded-back charge finishes inside the 5.4 to 6.6 hour timer, in the closed trunk, is still open.

The chip has no thermistor pin. The pack NTC is the Adafruit 372 bead already on the bill, with a window at 0.11 C and 59.80 C from its lookup table (calc). STAT is one open-drain pin to GPIO5 (header pin 29), pulled up to 3.3 V, no level-shifter IC. Enable is GPIO6 (header pin 31).

The nest folder, the netlist, the bill, and the decision log were not edited. Proposed merges are in `circuitry/CHARGER_BOARD.md`. `circuitry/check_charger.py` passed its checks. An electrical rule check of `circuitry/charger_board.yaml` returned 0 errors.
