# 2026-11-11 5:03 PM ET: the nest charging dock is decided (D61), it replaces the feet-contact dock (D58)

Labels: sim pass only (MuJoCo, the repo's sit model and the stock walk), calc only (arithmetic and estimates),
CAD check only (the sole's flat face). Nothing ran on hardware, nothing bought, no CAD yet.

- Ahmed approved the nest concept on 2026-11-11 ET ("I like that") and said "Yes go ahead" to recording the decision
  and starting the CAD. His brief: the mat is "Against the wall", no tags if software and the camera can do it, and
  "I want something that feels natural and the pad feels natural". DECISIONS.md D61 has the record; D58 carries a
  superseded note.
- The concept: a felt mat about 2 mm thick against the wall, a 10 cm golden cloth disc (plus) and a pale gold ring
  (minus), a soft rim at the wall for the plug and electronics, sun centre 190 mm from the wall (calc only). On the
  duck, four spring pins under the body: 2 in the seat block, 2 in the belly bumper, 130 mm apart (sim pass only).
  Nothing in the feet. The duck walks on, turns 180 deg, sits facing the room with the torque off (D59), later stands
  and walks straight out.
- Evidence copied into twin/dock/nest/ (three reports and 17 figures, local paths removed, no videos, no scripts):
  NATURAL_DOCK_IDEAS.md (19 concepts, pick A, runner-up B the pond rug), stripes/STRIPE_MAT_STUDY.md (why the feet
  and stripe route was dropped) and sim/NEST_SIM.md (the sequence sim).
- Sim result (sim pass only; the pose is a stand-in, not a camera): 540 runs; the first sit ends charging in 93.1 %
  (503), and in 98.4 % (503 of 511) of the runs that reached the sit. 29 falls, all at 7.2 V while steering across
  the 2 mm mat edge; straight crossings 80 of 80; no fall in the turn, the sit, the stand-up or the walk-off.
- Why the feet route was dropped: in the sit the soles are rolled about 8 deg (sim pass only) and the sole's flat
  face is only 84.8 x 15.8 mm (CAD check only), so the D58 pads do not fit; the striped mat needs 20 diodes and has
  a weaker safety case.
- What changes later, not done in this entry: D50's four nets become two and the MCP73213 charger moves into the duck
  (about 1.9 to 3.2 W of heat in the trunk, calc only). The netlists, the BOM and the 3D viewer still show the old
  wall and paddle dock.
- Open BLOCKERs are listed in D61 and STATE.md. The first one, the mat-edge falls when steering at 7.2 V, has a
  straight-crossing re-run in progress with no result yet.
- STATE.md has a new Nest dock section.
