# Thermal twin: knees reach the 70 C cut in continuous walking in sim, 25/75 duty does not; electronics and PLA heat checked (sim pass only)

- When: 2026-11-03 3:20 PM (America/New_York); work from about 2:05 PM
- Source: working tree of branch claude/digital-twin (no commit); new folder twin/thermal/ only; work by the thermal twin sub-agent for the lead Claude Code agent
- Written: 2026-11-03 by the Claude Code agent (thermal twin sub-agent)

- Why: sim/RUNTIME_THERMAL.md left the STS3215 thermal resistance as a BLOCKER, with the knees at 3 to 5.5 x their rated copper loss; Ahmed said a hot servo could mean a redesign.
- Data search (section 1 of twin/thermal/THERMAL_TWIN.md): no STS3215 thermal resistance or time constant is published by Feetech. Found: Robo9 bench curves of the 12 V STS3215 C018 (static hold 25 to 48 C in 10 min, steady to 60 min; oscillation to 60 C, then 71 C), the Robo9 five-servo comparison (two-minute 50 % load: STS3215 inside +10 C, case +3.5 C; STS3250 +25 C), an SO-100 gripper report (50 C within 30 to 60 s at a stall, one servo damaged over 70 C), the Feetech protection registers (70 C unload, 2.0 A for 2 s, 80 % for 2 s then 20 %), Faulhaber data for DC motors of the same size (winding to housing 6.9 to 10 K/W), and no heat reports in the Open Duck issues. Several hosts were not readable (feetech.cn, zhihu, csdn, the Poppy forum, the Discord).
- Model (twin/thermal/twin_thermal.py): heat per servo from the stock walk traces of our 2.65 kg build (copper with its temperature coefficient, board, gear friction, idle), two nodes per servo (winding and body), the measured outer case area and printed-part exposure of every servo (geometry.py on the CAD meshes), the trunk and head as air and wall nodes with their measured skins, the cells as a node, the BEC, Pololu 2815, fuse, BMS and 5 V regulator as hot spots; three parameter corners and one-at-a-time sweeps. The nominal pack uses the closed-loop currents of twin/electromech (states runs).
- Fit (fit_robo9.py): the Robo9 static curve gives a time constant of 316 s (231 to 435 s); the physics value for a bare servo body is 358 to 408 s. Only the C018 stall-line reading explains both Robo9 tests, and it puts the sensor close to the body (w = 0.03 to 0.19). The thermal resistance itself cannot be fitted (no heat input published): 7.1 to 9.2 K/W body to air for a walking knee, 11.2 to 11.6 K/W on a bench, from physics.
- Result, central corner, full pack: the hotter knee reaches the 70 C cut after about 10 min of continuous walking (plain model) and 4 min (backlash model) at 25 C, 7 and 3 min at 32 C; the winding is then 107 to 132 C. Stand and walk 50/50 peaks at 61 / 68 C (the backlash 50/50 cuts at 13 / 8 min); stand 75 %, walk 25 % peaks at 50 to 66 C. Low corner: no cut except the backlash walk at 32 C; high corner: cuts within 1 to 6 min everywhere. The over-current and overload timers never trip in the walk traces (longest runs 0.06 and 0.11 s against 2 s). A thermal guard that stands the robot at 55 C and walks again at 45 to 50 C, or rests with torque off, avoids every cut (12 to 38 walking minutes in 90 at 25 C).
- Electronics, central: 6.1 to 8.8 W in the trunk while walking (BEC 3.9 to 5.3 W at the assumed 85 %); at the battery stop trunk air 38 to 48 C, cells 44 to 56 C, BEC case 98 to 122 C, Pololu 2815 59 to 83 C, BMS 49 to 67 C, head air 34 to 41 C. Bench predictions: TEST_PLAN 4.1 passes only at about 92 % BEC efficiency; TEST_PLAN 4.2 (board under 50 C) is expected to fail at about 55 C.
- PLA: HDT 49 to 57 C, Tg 55 to 60 C (Overture Easy PLA gives Vicat 66.2 C only). The eight knee-to-ankle sheets see 54 to 66 C in continuous and 50/50 walking (47 to 55 C at 25/75); trunk_top and roll_to_pitch up to 61 C; the belly floor under the BEC and the battery_pack_lid at the 2815 and BMS above 50 C.
- TO1: holding 1.5 A as TEST_PLAN 7.3 now says reaches the 70 C cut in about 4 min in the twin; the proposed hold is 0.9 A to settle, then 1.2 A to 60 C, and the pass rule becomes the re-run twin.
