# Every-part BOM, harness build sheet, viewer pipeline for screws, cables and ties

- When: 2026-11-01 9:30 PM (America/New_York)
- Source: this commit, branch claude/every-part (PR 1 was merged into altaay-lab on Ahmed's ask before this work)
- Written: 2026-11-01 by the Claude Code agent

- Ahmed's ask: "I still have not seen every screw ... every cable ... a bill of materials where, for every part", and the exploded view must show every cable and strap.
- BOM_FULL.md and bom_full.csv (generated by viewer/bom_full.py through viewer/make_viewer_data.py --write-bom): one row per distinct item in the robot (168 rows, 405 pieces before the screw schedule), with where it goes, the viewer nodes that show it and the BOM.md line that supplies it. It found 8 needs with no purchase line (solder, cable ties, a USB-C PD supply for the iron, a USB-C supply to charge the robot, multimeter, bench supply, calipers, a load for the regulator tests); a sourcing pass is finding US lines for them.
- BOM.md Need values corrected from the netlist (totals unchanged, $837.14): Mini-SPOX housings 5 (was 2), terminals 13 (was 6), 16 AWG 1.1 m, 20 AWG 1.9 m, 22 AWG 1.9 m, 26 AWG 8.8 m.
- HARNESS_BUILD.md (wiring/make_harness_build.py): cable-by-cable build sheet for all 45 active cables and 118 wires: cut lengths, colours, the end treatment at each end, splices, and the continuity test for each cable. Bracket seats and ties are filled in from the 3D cable routing when it lands.
- Viewer pipeline (viewer/make_viewer_data.py): reads altaay_lab/fasteners/fasteners.json (screws as parts), altaay_lab/cables3d/cables3d.json (cables, terminal seats for the parts still in the tray, ties and guides), adds a purchase link per BOM line, and stale labels fixed (BEC Pro, fuse holder, 5 V regulator, speaker, SS-01 foot switches).
- sim/plot_power_path.py and sim/odm_v2_power_path_voltages.png: servo supply voltage over 6 s of walking from the ngspice model (star wiring D15, nominal pack; sim pass only). Re-run reproduced the documented worst case: right ankle 5.04 V (typical), 3.44 V (high-side corner).
