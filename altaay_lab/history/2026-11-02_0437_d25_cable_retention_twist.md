- One anchor per spot: the mount check looked at each tie point alone, so two mounts (or two tie-hole pairs) could sit on the same patch. In the committed file (05be03d) 15 mount pairs on the same face of the same link overlap for certain (centres 2.4 mm and more apart, closer than half their two widths added), and in this job's first run the T014 and T039 hole pairs came out 4.4 mm apart centre to centre. seats_ties.py now refuses a mount or a hole pair that overlaps one already placed on the same face (separating axis test in the plane, 1.6 mm round every hole), and a tie point whose own spot is taken joins the nearest anchor of its link within the dressing distance (20 mm at a loop end, 25 mm elsewhere): both bundles on one anchor, one tie round both. 15 points joined that way (listed as `also_holds`), so the file has 75 tie points instead of 90 (ids have gaps).

P13, D2 and S12 meet the rule over the full range (bend R 11.0, 10.5 and 11.3 mm against 10.8, 10.4 and 7.8 mm), so the full loops stay and no software limit is needed for the cables; a head yaw manual-move limit for the test plan is proposed to the lead.
