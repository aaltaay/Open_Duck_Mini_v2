- Build-blocking findings: (1) today's Raspberry Pi OS (Trixie, Python 3.13) cannot install the Runtime's pinned packages: use Bookworm 2026-10-06; Blinka must be pinned to 9.2.0 with RPi.GPIO 0.7.1; (2) pypot flushes before every send, which on the cdc-acm driver can cancel writes in flight; on the simulated bus the stock configure script left register 85 at 50 (the G1 symptom); our scripts drain, read every register back and fix it (real-adapter rate UNVERIFIED); (3) D16 cannot be applied literally while walking (servo voltage reads 6.0 V near 3.57 V per cell): walk stop on a 5 s mean at or under 6.0 V while walking, hard stop at 6.0 V standing or 5.4 V walking.
- Notes: the upstream 0.2.3 image starts an unguarded walk at boot (use the manual install); the Runtime's rustypot panic (G10) is reproduced and the guard turns torque off through pypot instead; the star-wiring sim predicts the TEST_PLAN 9 ankle gate fails at nominal charge (30 to 34 % of the walk under 6 V), so a power path follow-up is running.
