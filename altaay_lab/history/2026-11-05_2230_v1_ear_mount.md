# V1 track 11: the ear SG90 mount checked again; the viewer showed it wrong (CAD check only)

2026-11-05 about 10:30 PM ET (America/New_York). Adds to the track 11 entries; they are not edited.

Ahmed saw the two ear SG90s (the antenna servos, GPIO12 and 13) floating in the head with nothing holding them. An
earlier CAD check said they hang from the head plate, tabs flush on 7.25 mm of plastic, pilots concentric.

What the CAD has (twin/companion/ear_mount.py, ear_mount.json; CAD check only). Same numbers in print/head.stl, in
twin/companion/stl/head.stl (the copy with the mic seats) and in the decimated head mesh of the viewer GLB, with the
viewer's own SG90 pose (0.0000 mm from audit/assembly.json):
- each tab flat on 7.25 mm of plastic: 58.5 of 58.5 mm2 per tab within 0.15 mm, largest gap 0.00 mm;
- 2 blind pilots per servo, 1.49 mm, 4.7 mm to the drill tip, 27.5 mm apart, coaxial with the tab holes (axis 0.4 deg,
  centre 0.001 mm off);
- the body ends sit in the plate pocket: 117.7 mm2 within 0.15 mm, gap 0 to 0.1 mm at both ends. With the 2 screws
  this is what stops the servo turning under the horn load. The +y face (servo frame) has 12.0 mm to the wall, the
  other face is open; the body below the tabs touches nothing (19.8 mm or more to the shell at mid-body);
- the output shaft is on the ear joint axis (0.000 mm, 0.000 deg); over -90 to 90 deg the holder and rod clear the
  shell by 2.02 mm, the mic boards by 10.07 mm and the camera by 82.8 mm.
So the earlier check was right and no STL changes. No screw changes, no BOM change, the mic seats are untouched.

What was wrong is what the viewer shows:
- the 4 tab screws (F348 to F351) were hosted on the head shell ("head shell into head shell"), because the screw
  part name "SG90 tab" matched no part key, so they rode with the shell;
- the SG90s exploded on a radial vector of their own, the shell straight up 150 mm. At the default explode (0.35) each
  servo sat 22.2 mm (left) and 22.3 mm (right) off its plate with its screws left in the shell (calc: 0.118 x the
  188.4 and 188.9 mm vector differences); at full explode about 189 mm.
Fixed in viewer/make_viewer_data.py: the "SG90 tab" screws ride with their SG90 (label "SG90 ear servo tab into head
shell"); the SG90s, the antenna holders and rods ride with the head shell and part only along the ear axis
(EAR_PULL: servo 20 mm in, holder 30 mm out, rod 42 mm out, at full level-2 explode), so the stack servo, screws,
plate, holder stays on one line. The SG90 mount note now says where it hangs. Viewer data rebuilt and load-tested.

Also corrected: fasteners/FASTENERS.md finding 20 said the tab pilots are drilled along y, 20.7 deg off the tab axis.
That number is the feat3d fit, which reads every bore along a mesh axis; on the mesh the pilot is coaxial. make_fasteners
now measures the pilot axis on the mesh (pilot_axis_measured_deg); pilot_angle_deg keeps the fit angle for the site
bookkeeping. MOUNTING.md and COMPANION.md section 6 carry the numbers.
