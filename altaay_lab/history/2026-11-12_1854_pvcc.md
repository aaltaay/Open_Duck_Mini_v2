# 2026-11-12 6:54 PM ET: PVCC is 20 V, not 30 V

Cited from the BQ24133 absolute maximum table. The SMAJ16A clamp of 26.0 V is under the 30 V rating of AVCC and over the 20 V rating of PVCC. No SMAJ row stands off at 13.6 V and clamps at or under 20 V. PVCC sits after the datasheet's ACFET and RBFET, which open when OVPSET hits 1.6 V. That point is set at 18 V on the input (calc only), so the trip stays under 20 V and a 15 V supply does not open the FETs. The 1 µs deglitch is not shown to hold PVCC under 20 V. The MOSFET part is not chosen.
