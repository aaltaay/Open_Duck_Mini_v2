- bench/power_log.py --walk: the old log read registers 62 to 70 of all 14 servos inside every 20 ms cycle, before the Runtime's reads. Every goal write came one read later (3.0 ms on the simulated bus) and its count was a phase sample: 0.3 to 0.8 % for a dip that fills 19.7 % of the time. Now one read at a random time in each 27 ms slot runs only inside the Runtime's own end-of-cycle sleep (new bench/odm_bench/vsampler.py; walkhooks swaps the name `time` inside v2_rl_walk_mujoco only, no Runtime file edited). A read starts only if its expected time plus 2 ms ends before the next cycle; once started it runs to its end. The old log stays as `--sampler cycle`, for comparison only; it cannot score the gate.
- Why random in each slot and not every 27 ms: the step cycle is 27 control cycles (polynomial_coefficients.pkl, 0.54 s), so a fixed 27 ms timer reads the same 20 points of every step. Sim traces, 60 s: off by up to 10.9 points (sim pass only); test: 0.0 to 10.0 % for an exact 6.68 %, the random timer 5.9 to 7.0 %.
- Counting in time: per servo, per 1 ms bin of the time since the goal write, each bin weighted by its real share of the time between goal writes; bins the timer cannot read take the share of the last bins read (high, not low, while 9.recovery holds); each reading binned at its modelled latch time (latch instant UNVERIFIED; if earlier, the count errs high). Lead's decision applied: every servo is timed, gate 9.time6 = no servo over 10 % at nominal (ankles, knees, head roll named); 9.wiring compares ankles, knees, head roll and the lowest servo with the as-built high-side corner (new tests/data/sim_power_fix_reference.json from sim/POWER_PATH_SIM.md, sim pass only); `--feeds none` for a log with SP4/SP5 unplugged.
- Checked: virtual clock, dip of 19.80 % locked to the goal writes: 19.69, 19.66, 19.76 % at 55, 41, 26 % of the walk read (share of readings 33.6 to 77.4 %). Real walk loop, wire-timed simulated bus, idle: 19.0 to 22.5 % against 19.6 to 19.7 %, 44 to 48 % read.
